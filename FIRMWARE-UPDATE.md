# Steps

1. Turn off both sides
2. Connect the left side
3. Double click with a pin the reset button
4. Copy the reset file to the bootloader usb drive
5. After the reset is complete copy new firmware for the left side
6. Disconnect the left side without turning it on
7. Repeat steps 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 for the right side
8. Reconnect the left side
9. Turn on the right side

## Tips

ALWAYS connect/power-on the LEFT module FIRST, and disconnect/power-down the LEFT
module LAST. You NEVER want the RIGHT side to lose track of the LEFT and flash red.
